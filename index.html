Hei, I’m Sandesh

My journey, from teaching computers in Itahari to designing in Hämeenlinna.

I started as a computer trainer and freelance designer in Nepal, then spent two years at an education consultancy designing their social media, print and merch before moving into student counselling. In Finland I study Computer Applications at HAMK, designed LinkedIn content for a UAE market entry company and lead a sprint in a game project for people with memory disorders.

I like work that has to perform: posts read in a second, flyers that work from across the street, and screens that never confuse anyone.

Questions recruiters ask me

Which roles are you looking for?

Social media, content creation, visual design and junior UX. I’m also open to marketing or design internships and trainee roles.

Where are you based?

Hämeenlinna, Finland, where I study at HAMK.

Which tools do you use?

Figma for layout, systems and prototypes. Photoshop and Illustrator for brand and social work. Godot for game prototypes, plus AI image tools where they save time.

Can you work inside an existing brand?

Yes. At Oli & Associates I kept one look across posts, print and merch for two years, and at Omendon I built templates the team reused.

Can you do both social design and UX?

Yes. Social and brand is my daily work. For UX, I hold the Google UX Design Certificate and design the Memoera game with real user feedback.

Where can I see more work?

On Behance and LinkedIn, where I post new pieces.
